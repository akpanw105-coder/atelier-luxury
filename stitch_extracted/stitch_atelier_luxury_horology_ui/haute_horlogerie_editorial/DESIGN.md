## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the discipline, pedigree, and quiet authority of bespoke high horology. Designed for collectors, connoisseurs, and patrons of master watchmaking, the interface evokes the reverent atmosphere of a private salon or an haute horlogerie museum archive. 

The aesthetic marries classical editorial print with cinematic digital minimalism:
- **Cinematic Chiaroscuro:** Deep, obsidian architectural depths contrasted against warm ivory typography and selective champagne gold highlights.
- **Museum-Grade Precision:** Utter restraint in ornamentation. Form follows precision mechanics, relying on mathematical proportion, hairline platinum delineations, and deliberate negative space.
- **Tactile Serenity:** Interfaces respond smoothly and deliberate—never frenetic. Interactions mirror the weighty, silent sweep of a tourbillon movement rather than instant digital snapping.

## Colors

The chromatic palette is strictly rooted in raw horological materials: forged carbon, blackened steel, brushed platinum, and hand-burnished champagne gold.

- **Obsidian Black (`#08080A`):** The primary canvas ground. Absorbs light entirely, providing an infinite depth that allows timepiece photography and typography to float.
- **Charcoal (`#121215`):** Level 1 elevated surface for primary content containers, gallery backgrounds, and side panels.
- **Deep Slate Surface (`#1A1A1E`):** Level 2 surface for active cards, contextual dropdowns, and flyouts.
- **Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`, accents at `#D4AF37` and `#DFBA73`):** Reserved exclusively for high-significance states, active indicators, caliber badges, and subtle hover illuminations. Used with extreme restraint to prevent gaudiness.
- **Warm Ivory (`#F6F4EE`):** Primary display text, headlines, and hero copy. Softer and more tactile than cold digital white.
- **Platinum Silver (`#D1D3D8`):** Body typography, technical specs, and interactive controls.
- **Subdued Stone (`#8E8E93`):** Metadata, movement reference numbers, legal copy, and inactive indicator dots.
- **Hairline Platinum Dividers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` / `#26262B`):** Architectural boundary lines defining the grid without imposing weight.

## Typography

Typography establishes an aristocratic balance between romantic, classical editorial sensibilities and technical horological cataloging.

- **Display & Headline Expression:** Set in **Playfair Display**. It provides high-contrast transitional strokes reminiscent of Geneva watchmaking certificates and bespoke auction catalogs. Headlines rely on light weights (`400` and `500`) to maintain an airy, delicate presence.
- **Body & Spec Clarity:** Handled by **Plus Jakarta Sans** set at a refined `300` weight for long-form editorial essays, ensuring transparent legibility against dark canvases without chromatic vibration.
- **Uppercase Horological Labels:** All operational metadata (e.g., calibre references, complication indicators, case metallurgy) utilize uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` with expanded letter-spacing (`0.2em` to `0.25em`), echoing the precision engraving on movement baseplates and case backs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetric 12-column fixed-max-width grid (capped at `1440px` with fluid margins extending to infinity). Generous negative space gives each horological complication room to breathe.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `2rem` gutters and minimum `4rem` outer margins. Modules allow for dramatic editorial asymmetry—pairing full-bleed movement macro shots across 7 columns with 5 columns of quiet, center-aligned technical narrative.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2.5rem` outer margins. Content groups collapse into balanced vertical tiers.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer canvas padding. Full-width horizontal gallery swipes replace desktop asymmetric spreads, with strict vertical rhythm maintained via `space-lg` separation between curations.

## Elevation & Depth

Physical depth in this design system is achieved through tonal stratification and precise hairline delineations, strictly eschewing heavy blur dropshadows.

- **Tonal Layers:** Interfaces rise from the abyssal Obsidian ground (`#08080A`) to Charcoal (`#121215`) for contextual surfaces, and Deep Slate (`#1A1A1E`) for elevated floating panels or interactive tooltips.
- **Hairline Framing:** Depth boundaries are defined by `1px` borders in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. These borders represent micro-milled chamfers on fine watch cases.
- **Ambient Illumination:** When an elevated layer must cast separation over an image (such as an acquisition tray or an inspect modal), it uses an ambient, diffused glow rather than a black drop:
  - `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15);`
  The trace champagne hue mimics warm salon spotlighting grazing an exhibit display.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an uncompromising crisp geometry (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Sharp Edge Geometry:** Corners of cards, modal windows, layout containers, and primary buttons are executed with strictly unrounded `0px` corners. This captures the razor-sharp hand-beveled edges of movement bridges and architectural vitrines.
- **Circular Complications:** The single exception is functional dials, movement jewel indicators, and status points, which remain perfect geometric circles (`rounded-full`) to echo circular cases, bezels, and balance wheels.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Links
- **Primary Acquisition CTA:** Crisp rectangular boundary, background of `#C5A059`, label in `#08080A` set in `label-caps-sm`. On hover, transitions subtly to `#DFBA73` with a fine external border offset.
- **Ghost Salon Button:** Transparent container framed by a `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text in `#F6F4EE`. On hover, the border illuminates to `#C5A059` and text transitions to warm champagne.
- **Text Action:** Monospaced or tracked uppercase label accompanied by a hairline horizontal arrow (`->`), shifting 4px rightward on hover.

### Timepiece Cards
- Seamless, dark surface cards (`#121215`) with zero corner radius and an imperceptible `1px` perimeter line (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Timepiece photography is isolated or lit in chiaroscuro against pitch black. Hovering induces an ultra-slow, cinematic zoom (scale `1.03` over `700ms` cubic-bezier easing) accompanied by the gentle reveal of the reference number and calibre details.

### Navigation & Header
- Architectural floating top bar with an absolute blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`) over an 85% transparent `#08080A`.
- Divided from the viewport by an exact bottom hairline (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Navigation links set in `label-caps-sm`, with hover states highlighted by a centered `1px` champagne gold tick mark below the label.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Flat surfaces embedded into `#121215` with an underline-only or full hairline border.
- Placeholder text in `#8E8E93`. On focus, the bottom border shifts from subdued platinum to vibrant Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`), with zero glowing shadows.

### Specification Lists & Technical Grids
- Two-column spec modules for movement details (Diameter, Frequency, Power Reserve, Jewels).
- Left-aligned parameter in `#8E8E93` (`caption-specs`), right-aligned value in `#F6F4EE` (`body-sm`), divided by a dotted or hairline platinum baseline separator.
